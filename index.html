File Shrinker

Make videos, audio recordings and PDFs smaller. Everything runs in your own browser, and your files are never uploaded.

VideoMP4 CompressorPick a resolution and quality, or a target size such as 25 MB. AudioM4A CompressorShrink voice recordings, podcasts and songs to a compact M4A file. PDFPDF CompressorShrink heavy slide decks to fit an email. Text stays searchable.

Everything the tools need is stored on this site. Your files stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

Made by Rizky Wicaksono, public relations engineer. Free to use, nothing is uploaded. Source on GitHub.
