File Shrinker

Make videos, audio recordings and PDFs smaller. Everything runs in your own browser, and your files are never uploaded.

VideoMP4 CompressorPick a resolution and quality, or a target size such as 25 MB. AudioM4A CompressorShrink voice recordings, podcasts and songs to a compact M4A file. PDFPDF CompressorReduce scans and slide decks. Pages become pictures, so text is no longer searchable.

The tools load their code from public CDNs on first use, so you need an internet connection when you open them.
